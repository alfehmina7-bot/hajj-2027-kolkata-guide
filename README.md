## Official Routes

Indian pilgrims can travel only through:

1. **Haj Committee of India (HCoI):** the government route, with seats allotted by draw of lots (qurrah).
2. **Haj Group Organisers (HGOs):** government-approved private operators. The approved list is published on the HCoI website.

Avoid anyone offering a "guaranteed Hajj visa" outside these two channels.

## Eligibility Checklist

- [ ] Passport issued on or before the application deadline
- [ ] Passport valid until at least Dec 31, 2027
- [ ] Medical fitness certificate
- [ ] Required vaccinations
- [ ] Have not performed Hajj via HCoI before (exceptions: mehram for a female pilgrim, or companion for pilgrims aged 65+)

## Booking Steps

1. Confirm eligibility and passport validity.
2. Choose your route: HCoI waiting list or a private HGO.
3. Verify the operator against the official HCoI list.
4. Get the full package in writing: hotels, distance from the Haram, meals, flights, exclusions.
5. Complete medical tests and vaccinations.
6. Pay only through traceable channels and keep receipts.
7. Submit signed forms and documents on time.
8. Attend pre-departure training.

## Common Mistakes

- Paying before verifying registration
- Ignoring cancellation penalties (HCoI penalises cancellations other than for death or serious illness)
- Trusting "100% confirmed seat" claims
- Comparing only price, not facilities
- Forgetting to ask about Qurbani arrangements

## FAQ

**Are 2027 applications still open?**
The regular HCoI window has closed. Waiting-list rounds are ongoing, and private operators may have limited seats.

**Can I go without the Haj Committee?**
Yes, but only through an approved Haj Group Organiser.

**Where do I find official updates?**
[hajcommittee.gov.in](https://hajcommittee.gov.in) and the Haj Suvidha app.
